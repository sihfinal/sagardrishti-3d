# Demo Video Script (≤ 2:00)

Recording setup: `/` landing page, then `/explore`. Quality preset "High" on a
decent laptop; drop to "Medium" if the capture stutters. Voiceover lines in
quotes.

---

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem framing (landing page)

- Show the animated globe; let it rotate ~4 s before speaking.
- VO: "India's EEZ demands constant ocean monitoring, yet model output and
  real observations live in separate desktop tools. SagarDrishti-3D fuses them into
  one interactive 3D environment."
- Click **Launch 3D Explorer**.

## 0:20–1:00 — The living water volume

- Ocean Volume view loads; slow orbit across the block.
- Point at the surface: "Gridded temperature and salinity from NOAA's World
  Ocean Atlas — 25 depth levels rendered as one continuous volumetric block,
  with walls and seafloor derived from the same data."
- Select variable `Temperature · monthly`, press **Play**.
- While morphing: "The entire ocean breathes through the seasonal cycle —
  this is real climatology, not decoration."
- Drag the depth slider to ~200 m; toggle the isosurface at 20 °C briefly.

## 1:00–1:30 — Instruments + validation (the differentiator)

- Hover an Argo float: "Hovering compares the instrument's surface reading
  with the gridded field at that exact cell."
- Click a float — camera flies in; profile chart appears.
- Point at metrics row: "Observed profile against the field, with bias, RMSE
  and where in the column they diverge."
- Open the Assimilation Lens bar, press Play, speed 2×.
- VO: "Every observation becomes an evidence strip colored by model error —
  blue where the model runs cold, red where it runs warm — validated against
  425 real profiles."

## 1:30–1:50 — Controls + colorbar + extras

- Quick cuts: colorbar palette switch + log scale on salinity;
  vertical exaggeration sweep; sun elevation change;
  HYCOM current arrows toggle; transect at 10°N.

## 1:50–2:00 — Close

- Back to wide shot, cinematic orbit on.
- VO: "One platform: model fields, observations and their disagreement —
  in the browser, fully offline, ready for INCOIS operations."
- End card: SIH26067 · team repo link.
